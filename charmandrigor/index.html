Charm and Rigor — an anagram of Richard Morgan

Who is not being seen?
Who is not being heard?
Who is not being loved?
Who is not being helped?

I am a gay, Palestinian, immigrant journalist driven by these four questions to write stories at the triangulation of dignity, innovation, and surprise.

I am equally comfortable at LVMH's startup hub in Paris, beside the Queen's coffin in Westminster Abbey, at a research station in the Alaskan Arctic, refugee camps in Palestine, a wool farm in Australia, gay bars in Havana, foraging forests in Sweden, "the gayest place on Earth," the Incan ruins of Peru, helipad swordplay in Tokyo, pizza contests in Italy, or an olive oil wrestling tournament in the Turkish countryside. I have reported from dozens of countries — not always with permission and not always in English.

Before I was a journalist, I was a missionary in China, a ranch hand in Colorado, and a hitchhiker in Costa Rica.

Beyond the bylines

Beyond articles and essays, I've written a short memoir, Born in Bedlam, and been a major contributor to My First New York, a book by New York magazine, as well as a curator for Rolling Stone Cover to Cover: The First 40 Years. I've also written and edited for indie magazines including Gather Journal, Kill Screen, and the long-gone-but-forever-wonderful Topic.
